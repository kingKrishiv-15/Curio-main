Delete your Curio account

Curio · developed by Krishiv

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Curio and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Privacy and account.
  3. Tap Delete family account, enter your password, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Curio account”. We’ll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address and sign-in details
  • Your learner’s nickname and all learning progress (games, points, streaks, discoveries, skill levels)
  • Family preferences, notifications and weekly reports

Nothing is kept on our live systems. Copies in routine backups expire within 30 days.
