Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Applies to the Curio app on Android, iOS and the web

Curio is a learning app of short daily brain games for children, set up and managed by a parent or guardian. This policy explains what information Curio collects, why, and the choices you have. We’ve written it in plain language because the people reading it are busy parents.

The short version: a parent creates the account with their email. Children only ever need a nickname. We keep game progress so learning can continue across devices. We don’t show ads, we don’t sell data, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Curio is developed by Krishiv (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, contact us at krishivkedia@gmail.com.

We do not collect a child’s full name, photos, voice, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, or any payment information. Curio has no chat, no public profiles, and no way for children to contact other users.

Basic technical data (such as IP address and device type) is processed by our hosting provider when the app connects to our servers, as part of normal security and operation. We don’t use it to profile anyone.

3. Children’s privacy

  • Curio accounts are created and controlled by a parent or guardian, who confirms they are an adult with authority to agree for the child before the account is created.
  • Children are not asked for their email address or any contact details.
  • We collect only what is needed for the learning experience (a nickname and progress) and nothing more.
  • We do not show advertising, use third-party ad or analytics trackers, or sell or share children’s information.
  • A parent can review, export or delete their child’s information at any time (see section 7).

If you believe a child has created an account without a parent’s involvement, contact us and we will delete it.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, saving progress, syncing across devices.
  • To send the emails you need or ask for: sign-up and sign-in codes, password resets, and — only if you turn it on — a weekly progress summary.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we don’t make automated decisions about you or your child.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We use a small number of service providers who process data only on our instructions to run Curio:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and hosting.
  • Our email delivery provider — to send codes and the optional weekly email.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of users.

6. Security and storage

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as secure hashes; we can never see them.
  • Each family’s data is isolated: database rules allow a signed-in parent to access only their own family’s records.
  • Your information may be stored on servers outside your country, with our providers’ safeguards in place.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and export your data: in the app, go to Profile → Privacy and account → Export my account data.
  • Change your learner’s nickname and your family preferences anytime in the app.
  • Turn off reminders and the weekly email anytime in Family preferences.
  • Delete your account and all of your family’s data: in the app, go to Profile → Privacy and account → Delete family account, or see how to request deletion.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights (for example under the GDPR, India’s DPDP Act or COPPA). Contact us to exercise them; we’ll respond within 30 days.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your family’s data is permanently deleted from our live systems straight away; any copies in routine backups expire within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your family’s data in a meaningful way, we’ll ask you to agree again in the app.
